# Shortcuts

Any assignable key can save a grid region: digits `0-9` plus
`` ` `` `-` `=` `[` `]` `\` `;` `'` `,` `.` `/` — physical key codes, so they
work regardless of keyboard layout (`` ` `` and `~` are the same key).

## Assign

While the panels are open:

1. Press an **unassigned key** (or **Option+key** to reassign)
2. The footer shows *assigning* with the key's color chip
3. Your **next drag is saved to that key and applied** — one motion: the
   window moves immediately and the panel closes

By default assignments are **pinned** to the display whose panel you're on:
click display B's panel, press the key, drag — the shortcut belongs to B.
Settings → Shortcuts can switch assignments to **relative** (no display
binding; the shortcut follows the panel at apply time).

## Apply

Press an assigned key while the panels are open → the target window snaps to
the saved region **on its saved display**, even if that's not where the panel
is. Example setup: `1`/`2` = left/right halves of display A, `3` = left half
of display B.

## Reading the grid

- Each key gets a unique high-contrast color
- Keys belonging to the current display show a **colored chip** on their
  region's anchor cell — **hover a chip** to see its region outlined in a thin
  dashed line
- Keys bound to other displays are listed in the footer
  (*other displays: `3` DELL U2720Q …*) and still fire

## Managing

Shortcuts persist in `config.json` and are editable in
[Settings → Shortcuts](./settings): monitor dropdown, drag-in-mini-grid region
editor, and add-by-pressing-a-key. They can also be created and removed
through the [HTTP API](../reference/http-api/index.mdx) and
[MCP tools](../reference/mcp) — see
[Automating Vindue](./automating) for recipes.

:::note[Scope]
Shortcuts are *local* — they fire while the panels are open. *Global* named
shortcuts (fire anytime, no panel) are on the roadmap.
:::
